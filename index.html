⚔️ HOW TO PLAY SHADOW CRAWL

1
Turn-Based Tactical World: Enemies only take a step when you take a step. Take your time!
2
Bump-to-Attack Combat: Use WASD, Arrows, or the D-Pad. To attack an enemy, simply walk directly into them!
3
Satchel & Hotbar (1-5): Walk over potions, scrolls, and equipment to pick them up. Press 1 through 5 or click the slot to use/equip!
4
The Victory Goal: Take the stone stairs (>) down through 5 levels. Slay the Shadow Lord Malakor on Floor B5 and claim the Amulet of Yendor to win!
