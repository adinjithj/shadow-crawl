⚔️ HOW TO PLAY SHADOW CRAWL

1
Turn-Based Exploration: You control the brave Knight. The dungeon only takes a turn when you take a turn. Take all the time you need to plan your moves!
2
Movement & Combat (Bump-to-Attack): Use W A S D, Arrow Keys, or the on-screen ▲ ◀ ▶ ▼ D-pad. To attack an enemy, simply walk directly into them!
3
Gather Loot & Manage Satchel: Step over Potions, Scrolls, Weapons, Armor, or Gold. Press 1 through 5 (or click the slot) to drink a potion, cast lightning, or equip gear!
4
The Objective: Find the stone stairs in each floor to descend deeper. Reach Floor B5, defeat Shadow Lord Malakor, and claim the Amulet of Yendor to win!
