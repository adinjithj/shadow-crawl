⚔️ HOW TO PLAY SHADOW CRAWL

• Turn-Based Tactical Combat: Enemies only move when you take a step. Plan your movements carefully!

• Bump-to-Attack: Walk directly into an enemy tile with WASD or Arrow Keys to strike them with your equipped weapon.

• Satchel & Hotbar: Step over potions, scrolls, or equipment to pick them up into your 5-slot satchel. Press 1 through 5 to use or equip them.

• The Objective: Locate the stone stairs (>) in each floor to descend deeper. Slay Shadow Lord Malakor on Floor B5 and take the Amulet of Yendor to claim Victory!
